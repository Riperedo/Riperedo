# Ricardo Peredo-Ortiz 

I am a researcher specializing in **systems out of thermodynamic equilibrium**, with an emphasis on **amorphous solidification** and **soft matter materials**. My work bridges the gap between theoretical physics and computational modeling to understand complex material behaviors.

### 🔬 Research & Expertise
- **Theory:** Non-Equilibrium Self-Consistent Generalized Langevin Equation (NESCGLE) theory.
- **Focus:** Glass transitions, non-ergodicity, and structural/dynamic properties of complex systems.
- **Innovation:** Developing a **Scientific SaaS** to provide efficient alternatives to traditional molecular dynamics simulations using NESCGLE-based solutions.

### 🛠️ Technical Toolkit
- **Languages:** Julia (Primary), C/C++, Python.
- **Tools:** Docker, Scientific Computing Libraries, Numerical Solvers.
- **Approaches:** Theory derivation, computational simulations, and web-based scientific tools.

### 🚀 Featured Projects
- [**AnalyticalStructureFactors.jl**](https://github.com/Riperedo/AnalyticalStructureFactors.jl) - A Julia package for computing analytical static structure factors for soft matter and glassy systems.
- [**ModeCouplingTheory.jl**](https://github.com/Riperedo/ModeCouplingTheory.jl) - Contributions to a fast solver for integrodifferential equations in glass-forming systems.
- [**OZE-c-Solver**](https://github.com/Riperedo/OZE-c-Solver) - A C-based solver for the Ornstein-Zernike equation.
- [**Edi-Bot**](https://github.com/Riperedo/Edibot) - is a core part of my current research. It utilizes Evolutionary Computation (specifically Differential Evolution) to infer and fit the structural properties of liquids.
- [**NESCGLE.jl**] Only by request. Julia-based repository to solve the Non-Equilibrium Self-Consistent Generalized Langevin Equation theory.
